Questions we often hear

What does Ligas Consulting do?
Ligas Consulting is a software consultancy. We design and build enterprise web applications in Java and React, advise on and build with the Liferay platform, and train development teams in modern coding, deployment and operations practice.
Which technologies do you specialise in?
Java for back ends and web applications, React for front ends including micro-frontend architectures, Node.js, and the Liferay platform.
Do you offer Liferay consulting?
Yes. We know the Liferay platform in depth and advise on how to use it well in existing and upcoming projects. We also build on Liferay directly.
Can you train our development team?
Yes. Our training is shaped around your company and covers coding, deployment and operations, so your engineers can own and run what we build together.
Who have you worked with?
We have worked with Vodafone, Hays, Capita, Inchcape, Liferay, Proventeq and the Charity Commission for England and Wales.
Who will we work with?
With Miroslav Ligas, our founder and lead consultant. He is a solution architect with over 15 years of commercial experience in enterprise Java, React and Liferay, and he stays hands-on in every engagement.
